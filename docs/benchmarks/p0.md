## Positions

- 200 positions in `bench/positions/NNN.json` (`state` = `battle.toJSON()`, plus both teams, seeds and the input log).
- Teams: Showdown's `[Gen 9 Champions] Random Doubles Battle` generator, every set forced to level 50, brought 4 of 6 by a seeded random pick.
- Played 3-6 turns with seeded random legal choices (Mega allowed with probability 0.2). Kept only positions where both sides wait on a normal move request.
- Stat points: the generator's sets (11 per stat). The battler conversion uses EV = max(8·SP − 4, 0), IV 31.

## (c) Damage probes

**Showdown**: `battle.actions.getDamage(source, target, move, true)` for every (active attacker, any other active mon, damaging move in its move slots) triple in each restored position: 5819 triples per repetition. `getActiveMove` construction is excluded from the timing.

**battler-calc**: `simulate_move` on the same triples, exported by `bench/showdown/gen-calc-probes.js` with the position's field, side conditions, HP, boosts, status, ability and item. Input construction is excluded; by-name data lookups are included.

### battler-calc coverage (from `battler-calc/src` at this commit)

- **Doubles spread:** `Move.spread` applies ×3/4. The caller must set it; the calculator does not count targets.
- **Crits:** `Move.crit` applies ×3/2 and ignores unfavourable boosts.
- **Weather:** hooks for Rain, Harsh Sunlight, Extremely Harsh Sunlight, Sandstorm, Snow, Strong Winds (6 hooks); Utility Umbrella and Air Lock/Cloud Nine suppression are tested.
- **Terrain:** Electric, Grassy, Misty, Psychic (4 hooks).
- **Screens:** Reflect and Light Screen (×1/2, ×2/3 in doubles) and Aurora Veil via `Field.{attacker,defender}_side.conditions`.
- **Hooks by kind:** 28 ability, 66 item, 26 move, 19 condition, 3 status. Over the 200 positions' sets, 493 of 2400 abilities and 302 of 2394 items have a hook.
- **Not modelled:** the calculator takes names and state, not a battle; no Champions-mod rules (it follows battler's mainline data).
- **Agreement with Showdown (informational only):** one random Showdown roll falls inside the calc's [min, max] in 2650 of 5310 comparable probes. Inputs were not controlled (volatiles, Champions-mod differences, variable-power moves, crits, spread targeting), so this is not a correctness measurement.

## Notes and caveats

- Showdown rollouts start from the exact mid-game position; battler rollouts start from turn 1 of the same teams. The two are not equivalent workloads.
- battler team conversion (`bench/battler-bench/src/main.rs`) maps a few species names by hand (`-F` forms, Tauros-Paldea, Squawkabilly, Maushold-Four). All 200 positions build in the battle benchmark; the calc names are not all resolvable (see errors above).
- battler builds use the default engine options of `TestBattleBuilder` (team validation off, `log_time` off, random speed ties).
- The VM is shared; max values include scheduler noise.
